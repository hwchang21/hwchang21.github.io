Academic profile

Huaiwen Chang 常怀文

Assistant Researcher · Biostatistician

National Clinical Research Center for Aging and Medicine
Huashan Hospital, Fudan University
Shanghai, China

Huaiwen Chang is an assistant researcher whose work combines biostatistics, clinical epidemiology, and machine learning to address questions across the kidney-care continuum. His research focuses on early identification of kidney-function decline in older adults, tubular-function biomarkers, prognostic risk stratification in hemodialysis, and data-informed support for incremental dialysis decisions.

Research

Questions grounded in clinical practice.

The research program links population-based cohorts with hospital real-world data, using methods appropriate to the clinical question and the maturity of the evidence.

01

Kidney function and aging

Clinically interpretable risk stratification using eGFR, albuminuria, and tubular-function measures in heterogeneous older populations.

02

Clinical prediction

Development and validation of prediction models with close attention to calibration, transparency, and intended clinical use.

03

Hemodialysis care

Longitudinal analysis of routinely collected laboratory and dialysis-session data to study outcomes and individualized treatment decisions.
